EVERYTHING YOU NEED
TO KNOW.

Find quick answers to common questions about Prime Fitness memberships, coaching, and facilities.

What are the exact gym opening hours at Arkaç Mall?

Prime Fitness is open 7 days a week from 06:00 in the morning until 22:00 at night, allowing you to train before work, during the afternoon, or after your workday.

Where exactly is Prime Fitness located?

We are located on the 3rd Floor of the Arkaç (Arkach) Shopping and Entertainment Center on Magtymguly Avenue (formerly Svoboda) in Ashgabat, Turkmenistan.

How do I book a 1-on-1 Personal Trainer?

You can fill out the online enquiry form on this page, message us on WhatsApp at +993 65 720791, or visit the club reception at Arkaç Mall. We will assign the ideal coach based on your specific goals.

Are locker and shower facilities included with memberships?

Yes, all active gym members have complimentary access to our modern, secure lockers, changing rooms, and private hot shower facilities.

What is the Prime 60-Day Transformation Challenge?

The Prime Challenge is our signature body transformation program combining tailored workout programming, body fat InBody scans, nutritional milestones, and constant coach accountability.
